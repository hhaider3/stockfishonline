# Chess piece artwork

The SVG pieces in `cburnett/` are the standard "cburnett" chess set:

- Author: [Colin M.L. Burnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:Cburnett)
- License: [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)
  (also available under GFDL/BSD/GPL; CC BY-SA chosen here)
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_chess_pieces
  (same files as distributed with [lichess](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila) in `public/piece/cburnett`)

Files are named `{color}{Piece}.svg` with color `w`/`b` and piece
`P`/`N`/`B`/`R`/`Q`/`K`, on a 45×45 viewBox.
